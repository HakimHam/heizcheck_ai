# Rental management only, no WEG in the MVP

HeizCheck's MVP serves Property Managers of rental buildings (Mietverwaltung), not condominium managers (WEG-Verwaltung), even though "defending statements at the owners' meeting" was part of the original vision. The two settings have different legal relationships. §556 BGB and CO2KostAufG govern landlord ↔ tenant, while WEG allocates to unit owners through the annual statement (Jahresabrechnung). Covering both would double the Ruleset. WEG support will also bring a Sign-off concept (a Check Report formally approved for the owners' meeting) that rental management doesn't need.

## Consequences

- The Ruleset and glossary assume a Tenant as the final recipient. Adding WEG means introducing a Unit Owner and a separate set of rules, not just extending this one.
