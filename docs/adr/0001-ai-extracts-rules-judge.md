# AI extracts, deterministic rules judge

The AI only turns a Statement Package's PDFs into structured figures. Deterministic rules decide whether anything is wrong. A Property Manager has to defend each finding to a Metering Provider or a Tenant by pointing to a specific paragraph of the law, and an LLM's judgement can't be defended that way. The consequence is that extraction becomes the part that has to be trusted: the Property Manager confirms the extracted figures before the rules run.

## Considered Options

- **AI extracts and judges**: rejected. Its findings wouldn't be reproducible or traceable to a paragraph of the law.
